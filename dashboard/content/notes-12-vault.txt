.trap **Traps.** Uncorrelated $\Rightarrow$ independent only for jointly normal pairs or two 0/1 variables. Pairwise $\Rightarrow$ mutual independence: false. Mutually exclusive $\Rightarrow$ independent: false unless a probability is 0. “The Cauchy mean is 0”: false. a.s. $\Rightarrow$ mean square: false. $d\Rightarrow P$ only when the limit is a constant. A sum of $\chi^2$ variables is $\chi^2$ only with independence. Normal marginals $\Rightarrow$ BVN: false. $R_{1.23}\ge r_{23}$: false. “Consistent $\Rightarrow$ UMP”: false. Before any arithmetic check the convention: geometric and negative binomial (trials or failures), exponential (rate or mean), gamma (rate or scale).
