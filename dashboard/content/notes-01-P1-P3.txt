@grp Axioms and their consequences
-* Axioms on a $\sigma$-field: $P(A)\ge0$, $P(\Omega)=1$, countable additivity. **Uncountable additivity is false:** under $U(0,1)$ every point has probability 0, yet the union has probability 1.
- Countable additivity $\iff$ finite additivity + continuity at $\varnothing$. Continuity: $A_n\uparrow A\Rightarrow P(A_n)\uparrow P(A)$ and $A_n\downarrow A\Rightarrow P(A_n)\downarrow P(A)$.
- Union of three: $\sum P(A)-\sum P(AB)+P(ABC)$. Exactly one of $A,B$: $P(A)+P(B)-2P(AB)$.
- With $S_k$ = sum of all $k$-fold intersections,
$$P(\text{exactly }m)=\sum_{k\ge m}(-1)^{k-m}\binom km S_k,\qquad P(\text{at least }m)=\sum_{k\ge m}(-1)^{k-m}\binom{k-1}{m-1}S_k.$$
- Boole: $P\left(\bigcup A_i\right)\le\sum P(A_i)$. Bonferroni: $P\left(\bigcap A_i\right)\ge\sum P(A_i)-(n-1)$.
- Bounds: $\max\{0,P(A)+P(B)-1\}\le P(AB)\le\min\{P(A),P(B)\}$ and $\max\{P(A),P(B)\}\le P(A\cup B)\le\min\{1,P(A)+P(B)\}$.
-* Independent events: $P\left(\bigcup A_i\right)=1-\prod(1-P(A_i))$. [2018 Q1] with $\tfrac12,\tfrac14,\tfrac18,\tfrac1{16}$: $1-\tfrac{315}{1024}=$ **709/1024**.
-! $P(A)=0$ does not force $A=\varnothing$; $P(A)=1$ does not force $A=\Omega$ (“almost surely”).
-! A $\sigma$-field on a finite $\Omega$ has $2^k$ members, never 3, 5 or 6. $\sigma(A)=\{\varnothing,A,A^c,\Omega\}$; two generic events generate 16 sets.
-! The classical definition needs finitely many equally likely outcomes, and it is circular (“equally likely” already means equally probable).
- [2024 Q31] $P(A)=\tfrac13$, $P(B)=\tfrac15$, $P(A\mid B)+P(B\mid A)=\tfrac23$. Then $P(AB)(5+3)=\tfrac23$, so $P(AB)=\tfrac1{12}$ and $12\,P(\bar A\cup\bar B)=12\cdot\tfrac{11}{12}=$ **11**.

@grp Stored results for counting stems
-* Two named people adjacent: $2/n$ in a row, $2/(n-1)$ at a round table.
- Exactly $k$ people between A and B at a round table of $n$ ($k<(n-2)/2$): $2/(n-1)$. [2021 Q29] $n=16,k=4$: **2/15**.
- $2n$ boys and $2n$ girls split into two equal batches, each half boys: $\binom{2n}{n}^2\big/\binom{4n}{2n}$ [2022 Q8].
- $N$ items with $d$ defective inspected one by one: $P(\text{last defective at draw }k)=\binom{k-1}{d-1}\big/\binom Nd$. [2018 Q2]: $\binom83\big/\binom{15}4=$ **8/195**.
- Matching $n$ letters to $n$ envelopes: $P(\text{no match})=\sum_{k=0}^n(-1)^k/k!\to e^{-1}\approx0.368$; $P(\text{exactly }k)\to e^{-1}/k!$; **the expected number of matches is 1 for every $n$**.
-! Birthday: $P(\text{a shared birthday among }n)=1-\prod_{i=1}^{n-1}(1-i/365)$; $n=23$ gives 0.507. “Someone shares *my* birthday” needs $n=253$.
- de Méré: at least one six in 4 throws 0.518; at least one double six in 24 throws 0.491.
- Coupon collector: expected draws to see all $n$ types $=n\left(1+\tfrac12+\cdots+\tfrac1n\right)$; a die needs 14.7 throws.
- Two integers chosen at random are coprime with probability $6/\pi^2\approx0.608$.
- Ballot: A polls $a$, B polls $b$, $a>b$: $P(\text{A strictly ahead throughout})=(a-b)/(a+b)$.
- Tickets $1,\dots,2n+1$, three drawn: $P(\text{AP})=\tfrac{3n}{4n^2-1}$. Tickets $0,\dots,6n-1$, three drawn: $P(\text{sum}=6n)=\tfrac{3n}{(6n-1)(6n-2)}$.
- $ax^2+bx+c=0$ with $a,b,c$ from three dice: $P(\text{real roots})=43/216$.
- [2022 Q4] readers split 50/30/20%, six sampled, pattern 3–2–1: $\tfrac{6!}{3!\,2!\,1!}(0.5)^3(0.3)^2(0.2)=$ **27/200**.

.trap **Traps.** Mutually exclusive events with positive probabilities are never independent. The classical formula cannot be used on an infinite sample space. Method for areas: draw the rectangle of $(X,Y)$, shade the favourable side of the curve, integrate the short way.

@grp Machinery
-* $P(A\mid B)=P(AB)/P(B)$, defined only for $P(B)>0$. For fixed $B$ it is a full probability measure, so $P(A\mid B)+P(\bar A\mid B)=1$, but $P(A\mid B)+P(A\mid\bar B)$ need not be 1.
- Multiplication rule: $P(A_1A_2\cdots A_n)=P(A_1)P(A_2\mid A_1)\cdots P(A_n\mid A_1\cdots A_{n-1})$.
-! Total probability needs a partition (exhaustive **and** mutually exclusive): $P(A)=\sum_iP(A\mid B_i)P(B_i)$.
-! Truncated support: intersect first, then divide. [2026 Q16] $X\sim U(0,12)$: $P(5<X<7\mid X>6)=\tfrac{1/12}{1/2}=$ **1/6**.
-* Symmetry: without replacement, $P(\text{the }k\text{-th draw is red})$ equals the initial red proportion for every $k$.
- Pólya urn (return the ball plus $c$ of its colour): the next draw keeps the initial proportion. [2022 Q42] **3/7**.
- Equal priors: the posterior is just the normalised likelihoods.
$$P(B_j\mid A)=\frac{P(A\mid B_j)\,P(B_j)}{\sum_iP(A\mid B_i)\,P(B_i)}$$

@grp Independence
-* $A\perp\!\!\!\perp B$ implies $A\perp\!\!\!\perp\bar B$, $\bar A\perp\!\!\!\perp B$ and $\bar A\perp\!\!\!\perp\bar B$.
-* An event of probability 0 or 1 is independent of every event; $A\perp\!\!\!\perp A$ iff $P(A)\in\{0,1\}$.
- Mutual independence of $n$ events needs $2^n-n-1$ product conditions.
-! Pairwise $\not\Rightarrow$ mutual (Bernstein): two fair coins, $A$ = first head, $B$ = second head, $C$ = exactly one head. Each pair is independent, yet $P(ABC)=0\ne\tfrac18$.
-! The triple product rule alone does not give pairwise independence.
-! Conditional independence and independence imply neither each other (a fair or a two-headed coin, picked at random).
- [2025 Q11] table $0.4,0.1$ over $0.2,0.3$: $P(A)P(B)=0.3\ne0.4$, so dependent; $P(A\mid\bar B)+P(\bar A\mid B)=0.25+0.33>\tfrac12$. Answer **(b)**.

@grp CDF facts
-* $F(x)=P(X\le x)$ is non-decreasing, right-continuous, $F(-\infty)=0$, $F(\infty)=1$; any function with these four properties is a CDF.
-* Jump = point mass: $P(X=x)=F(x)-F(x^-)$ and $P(X<x)=F(x^-)$.
-* $P(a<X\le b)=F(b)-F(a)$; $P(a\le X\le b)=F(b)-F(a^-)$; $P(a<X<b)=F(b^-)-F(a)$; $P(a\le X<b)=F(b^-)-F(a^-)$.
- A CDF has at most countably many jumps.
-! Continuous $F$ does not guarantee a density (Cantor law). Every $F$ = discrete + absolutely continuous + singular part.
-! A pdf may exceed 1 ($U(0,\tfrac12)$ has $f=2$) or be unbounded ($\mathrm{Beta}(\tfrac12,\tfrac12)$); a pmf never exceeds 1.
- Quantile $\xi_p$: $F(\xi_p^-)\le p\le F(\xi_p)$. The median is not unique where $F$ is flat at $\tfrac12$.
- Symmetry about $a$ (continuous): $F(a-x)+F(a+x)=1$.
- $F$ continuous $\Rightarrow F(X)\sim U(0,1)$; for any $F$, $F^{-1}(U)$ has CDF $F$.
-! $X$ and $-X$ can share a law without $X=0$: equal in distribution is not equal.
- A joint CDF also needs the rectangle inequality $F(b,d)-F(a,d)-F(b,c)+F(a,c)\ge0$.

@grp Normalising constants in ten seconds
$$\int_0^1x^{m-1}(1-x)^{n-1}dx=B(m,n)=\frac{\Gamma(m)\Gamma(n)}{\Gamma(m+n)},\qquad \int_0^\infty\frac{x^{m-1}}{(1+ax)^{m+n}}dx=\frac{B(m,n)}{a^m}$$
$$\int_0^\infty x^ne^{-ax}dx=\frac{n!}{a^{n+1}},\qquad \int_0^\infty e^{-ax^2}dx=\frac12\sqrt{\frac\pi a},\qquad \int_0^\infty xe^{-x^2}dx=\frac12,\qquad \Gamma(\tfrac12)=\sqrt\pi$$
- Also $B(m+1,n)=B(m,n)\cdot\tfrac{m}{m+n}$ and $\Gamma(n+1)=n\Gamma(n)$.
@grp Moments straight from F
-* Any $X$: $\mathbb{E}X=\int_0^\infty(1-F)\,dx-\int_{-\infty}^0F\,dx$. For $X\ge0$: $\mathbb{E}X^r=\int_0^\infty rx^{r-1}\{1-F(x)\}\,dx$.
-* Non-negative integers: $\mathbb{E}X=\sum_{k\ge0}P(X>k)$ and $2\sum_kk\,P(X>k)=\mathbb{E}[X(X-1)]=\sigma^2+\mu^2-\mu$ ([2023 Q14] **(b)**).
