@sec P6 | Joint, marginal and conditional distributions | Probability
@meta codes=P6 pyq=40 f27=4.8 tier=1
@lede P6 is the heaviest sub-topic (40 PYQs, about 5 in 2027), and recent papers hang two or three questions on one density, so one clean workflow collects them all.
@grp One density, six steps
- **1.** Sketch the support and write each variable’s limits in terms of the other.
- **2.** Normalise with a Beta or Gamma integral.
- **3.** Marginal: $f_X(x)=\int f(x,y)\,dy$ over the slice at $x$.
- **4.** Conditional: $f(y\mid x)=f(x,y)/f_X(x)$, a density in $y$ on that slice.
- **5.** Moments: $\mathbb{E}(Y\mid x)$, $\mathbb{E}(Y^2\mid x)$, then $\mathbb{E}(XY)$ directly from $f$.
- **6.** Region probability: integrate over support $\cap$ region in the easier order.
@grp Independence tests
-* $f(x,y)=g(x)h(y)$ on a rectangle $\Rightarrow$ independent. **A non-rectangular support $\Rightarrow$ dependent, whatever the formula** ($f=2$ or $8xy$ on $0<x<y<1$).
- [2024 Q32] $c\,xy\,e^{-(x^2+y^2)}$ on $x,y>0$: $c=4$; $X^2,Y^2$ are iid $\mathrm{Exp}(1)$, so $P(X^2>2Y^2)=\tfrac13$; independent. **All three.**
- [2018 Q5] $(1+xy)/4$ on the square $[-1,1]^2$: $X,Y$ dependent, yet $X^2$ and $Y^2$ independent. **2 only.**
- [2018 Q15] a table with $\mathrm{Cov}=0$ but $P(X=0,Y=1)=0\ne\tfrac13\cdot\tfrac13$: uncorrelated, dependent. **2 only.**
-! Uncorrelated $\iff$ independent holds for two 0/1 variables and inside a jointly normal pair, not in general ($Y=X^2$ with symmetric $X$; uniform on a disc).
-! Normal marginals do not make the pair bivariate normal. The bivariate Cauchy $\tfrac{c}{2\pi(c^2+x^2+y^2)^{3/2}}$ has Cauchy$(0,c)$ marginals but is not their product.
- Fréchet bounds: $F_X(x)+F_Y(y)-1\le F(x,y)\le\min\{F_X(x),F_Y(y)\}\le\sqrt{F_XF_Y}$.

@grp Region tricks
.key For iid continuous $X,Y$: $P(X<Y)=\tfrac12$ and $P(X=Y)=0$; for iid exponentials $\lvert X-Y\rvert$ has the same exponential law; on a triangle, split the $x$-range where the upper limit changes.

@sec P7 | Functions of random variables | Probability
@meta codes=P7 pyq=15 f27=1.4 tier=2
@lede The same dozen results recycle; recognise the input family and the answer is a lookup, not an integral (15 PYQs, about 1.5 in 2027).
@grp Change-of-variable toolkit
- Monotone $g$: $f_Y(y)=f_X\left(g^{-1}(y)\right)\left\lvert\tfrac{d}{dy}g^{-1}(y)\right\rvert$. Several branches: add them.
- Square: $Y=X^2$ gives $f_Y(y)=\tfrac{f_X(\sqrt y)+f_X(-\sqrt y)}{2\sqrt y}$, $y>0$.
- Two variables: $f_{U,V}(u,v)=f_{X,Y}\left(x(u,v),y(u,v)\right)\left\lvert\tfrac{\partial(x,y)}{\partial(u,v)}\right\rvert$; the inverse Jacobian is $1/\lvert\text{forward Jacobian}\rvert$.
- Linear map $\mathbf u=A\mathbf x$: $g(\mathbf u)=f(A^{-1}\mathbf u)/\lvert\det A\rvert$. [2020 Q38] $A=\tfrac12\begin{pmatrix}1&1&0\\0&1&1\\1&0&1\end{pmatrix}$ has $\det A=\tfrac14$, so $g=$ **4f(u−v+w, u+v−w, −u+v+w)**.
- Convolution: $f_{X+Y}(z)=\int f_X(x)f_Y(z-x)\,dx$, limits from both supports ($0<x<z$ for positive variables).

@sec P8 | Expectation and conditional expectation | Probability
@meta codes=P8 pyq=24 f27=2.5 tier=1
@lede Almost every expectation item is one identity away: linearity, the tower rule, total variance, or the check “does $\mathbb{E}\lvert X\rvert$ converge?” (24 PYQs, about 2.5 in 2027).
@grp Does the mean exist?
-* $\mathbb{E}X$ exists iff $\mathbb{E}\lvert X\rvert<\infty$. A conditionally convergent $\sum x\,p(x)$ is not a mean.
- [2021 Q38] $X=(-1)^{i+1}2^i/i!$ with probability $2^{-i}$: absolutely convergent, $\mathbb{E}X=\sum(-1)^{i+1}/i!=$ **1−e^{−1}**.
-! Contrast: $X=(-1)^{k+1}3^k/k$ with probability $2/3^k$ has $\sum\lvert x\rvert p=2\sum\tfrac1k=\infty$, so no mean, although $\sum xp=2\ln2$.
- Lyapunov: $(\mathbb{E}\lvert X\rvert^r)^{1/r}$ is non-decreasing in $r$, so a higher moment implies every lower one ([2022 Q55] **2 only**). Also $\beta_k^2\le\beta_{k-1}\beta_{k+1}$ for absolute moments.
- Tails decide: $t_n$ has moments of order $<n$, Pareto($\alpha$) of order $<\alpha$, Cauchy none; St Petersburg has $\mathbb{E}=\infty$.
@grp Linearity and variance algebra
-* $\mathbb{E}(aX+bY)=a\mathbb{E}X+b\mathbb{E}Y$ with no independence needed. $\mathrm{Var}(aX+bY)=a^2\sigma_X^2+b^2\sigma_Y^2+2ab\,\mathrm{Cov}(X,Y)$.
- Model: $\mathrm{Var}X=4$, $\mathrm{Var}Y=9$, $\mathrm{Cov}=1$: $\mathrm{Var}(2X-3Y)=16+81-12=$ **85**.
- [2021 Q28] $4\sigma_1^2+9\sigma_2^2=72$ and $\sigma_1^2+4\sigma_2^2=25$ give $\sigma_1^2=9$, $\sigma_2^2=4$; $\mathrm{Var}\left(\tfrac23X_1-4X_2\right)=4+64=$ **68**.
- [2024 Q18] $\sigma_x^2=\tfrac{25}4$, $\sigma_y^2=\tfrac{25}9$: $\mathrm{Var}\left[(2x+3y)/\sqrt2\right]=\tfrac{25+25}{2}=$ **25**.
- [2024 Q19] $\mathrm{Cov}=2$, $\sigma_x=4$, $\sigma_y=9\sqrt7$: $(18r)^2=$ **1/7**.
- [2018 Q32] $U=X_1+X_2+X_3$, $V=X_1+X_2+X_4$, uncorrelated with equal variance: $\rho=$ **2/3** (shared terms over total terms).
- Indicators: $\mathbb{E}(\text{number of events})=\sum P(A_i)$. Fixed points of a random permutation: mean 1 and variance 1.
-! Independence gives $\mathbb{E}XY=\mathbb{E}X\,\mathbb{E}Y$; the converse fails. $\mathbb{E}(X/Y)\ne\mathbb{E}X/\mathbb{E}Y$; for independent positive $Y$, $\mathbb{E}(X/Y)=\mathbb{E}X\,\mathbb{E}(1/Y)\ge\mathbb{E}X/\mathbb{E}Y$.
- Jensen (convex $g$): $\mathbb{E}g(X)\ge g(\mathbb{E}X)$, so $\mathbb{E}(1/X)\ge1/\mathbb{E}X$, $\mathbb{E}\ln X\le\ln\mathbb{E}X$, HM $\le$ GM $\le$ AM. Cauchy–Schwarz: $(\mathbb{E}XY)^2\le\mathbb{E}X^2\,\mathbb{E}Y^2$.
- $\max(x,y)=\tfrac{x+y+\lvert x-y\rvert}2$, $\min(x,y)=\tfrac{x+y-\lvert x-y\rvert}2$; $\mathbb{E}\max\ge\max(\mathbb{E}X,\mathbb{E}Y)$, $\mathbb{E}\min\le\min(\mathbb{E}X,\mathbb{E}Y)$.
- Chebyshev’s association: $f,g$ monotone in the same direction $\Rightarrow\mathbb{E}[f(X)g(X)]\ge\mathbb{E}f(X)\,\mathbb{E}g(X)$; opposite directions reverse it.
- iid positive $X_i$: $\mathbb{E}(S_m/S_n)=m/n$.
@grp Conditional expectation
$$\mathbb{E}\left[\mathbb{E}(X\mid Y)\right]=\mathbb{E}X,\qquad \mathrm{Var}X=\mathbb{E}\left[\mathrm{Var}(X\mid Y)\right]+\mathrm{Var}\left[\mathbb{E}(X\mid Y)\right],$$
$$\mathbb{E}S_N=\mathbb{E}N\,\mathbb{E}X,\qquad \mathrm{Var}\,S_N=\mathbb{E}N\ \mathrm{Var}\,X+\mathrm{Var}\,N\,(\mathbb{E}X)^2\quad(\text{random sum, }N\perp\!\!\!\perp X_i).$$
-* $\mathbb{E}(X\mid Y)$ is a random variable, a function of $Y$. Both parts of the variance split are $\ge0$.
- Taking out what is known: $\mathbb{E}[g(Y)X\mid Y]=g(Y)\mathbb{E}(X\mid Y)$.
-! Independence $\Rightarrow$ mean independence $\Rightarrow$ uncorrelated, and neither arrow reverses.
- $\mathbb{E}(Y\mid X)$ minimises $\mathbb{E}[Y-g(X)]^2$ (the regression function); it equals the best linear predictor under joint normality.
- iid symmetry: $\mathbb{E}(X_1\mid\sum X_i)=\bar X$.
