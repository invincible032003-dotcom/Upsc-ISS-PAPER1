HOW TO OPEN
-----------
Double-click  index.html

That is the whole installation. The application runs in your browser
straight from the folder. There is nothing to install, no internet
connection needed, no server, no app store, no account.

Keep these four files together in one folder:

    index.html      the application (includes the LaTeX typesetting engine)
    questions.js    the question database - 720 authentic PYQs plus a
                    separate bank of 750 AI-generated 2027 forecast problems
    styles.css      the styling
    README.txt      this file

To move it to another device (phone, tablet, another computer), copy
the whole folder across and open index.html there.

- Windows / macOS / Linux : double-click index.html.
- Android tablet          : copy the folder to internal storage, open a
                            file manager, tap index.html, choose your
                            browser. (Tested layout: Galaxy Tab A9.)
- iPad / iPhone           : copy the folder into the Files app, tap
                            index.html. If your browser refuses to open a
                            local file, open the folder in Files, long-press
                            index.html and choose Share > your browser.


WHAT IS IN IT
-------------
720 authentic previous-year questions - the complete objective papers for
2018, 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025 and 2026, 80 questions each.

Every question keeps its original wording, its original four options in
the printed order, and its original question number.

Split by syllabus unit:

    Probability                                196
    Statistical Methods                        164
    Numerical Analysis                         180
    Computer Application and Data Processing   180
    ------------------------------------------------
    Total                                      720

Each question also carries a correct answer, an Exam Shortcut (a fast
~30-second method), a set of Tips & Tricks, and a full Step-by-Step
Solution.


IMPORTANT - WHERE THE ANSWERS COME FROM
---------------------------------------
The QUESTIONS are authentic. They are reproduced word for word from the
nine source papers, and nothing has been rewritten, simplified or
reordered.

The ANSWERS AND EXPLANATIONS ARE NOT OFFICIAL. None of the nine source
booklets carried an official UPSC answer key. Every correct answer, Exam
Shortcut, Tips & Tricks note and Step-by-Step Solution in this app was
worked out for this project and is labelled inside the app as an
"AI-derived explanation". Treat them as a well-checked study aid, not as
an official key.

Thirteen questions have defects in the printed source paper (a density
that does not integrate to 1, options missing from the scan, a statement
set that is not offered as an option, and so on). None of these has been
silently corrected. The original text is shown exactly as printed and the
problem is described on the question itself and listed in full under
Home > Data Audit. Two of them have no valid option at all; those two are
excluded from scoring rather than guessed.

The 720 PYQ items are all real. Nothing in that bank is generated, padded or
invented.


THE 2027 FORECAST BANK - AI-GENERATED, NOT PYQs
-----------------------------------------------
Alongside the 720 authentic PYQs the app carries a SECOND, SEPARATE bank of
750 forecast problems written for the 2027 attempt:

    Probability                                375
    Statistical Methods                        375
    ------------------------------------------------
    Total                                      750

THESE ARE NOT PREVIOUS-YEAR QUESTIONS. Every stem, every option, every
answer and every exam shortcut in the forecast bank was written for this
project. Nothing there is reproduced from a UPSC paper and no official UPSC
key exists for any of it.

The separation is enforced, not merely stated:

  - the two banks live in different arrays inside questions.js
    (window.quizData for the PYQs, window.forecastData for the forecast);
  - forecast ids look like FP-001 and FS-001, never like 2023-Q12;
  - a forecast question is badged FORECAST - AI-generated wherever it
    appears, and its explanations say so again;
  - Full Year, Sectional, Topic, Subtopic and Custom mocks are built only
    from the PYQ bank and can never serve a forecast question;
  - the four PYQ PDFs contain no forecast item and the two forecast PDFs
    contain no PYQ.

FORECAST MOCKS   Home > Forecast practice. Thirty named mocks:

    Probability Forecast Mock 01 ... 15
    Statistical Methods Forecast Mock 01 ... 15

Each holds EXACTLY 25 questions, each question appears in exactly one mock,
and every mock is spread across as many topics as the bank allows. Each can
be started in Learning Mode or in Strict Exam Mode, and both modes behave
exactly as they do for the PYQ mocks - including the fixed Learning Mode
reveal order.


THE MAIN MODES
--------------
STRICT EXAM MODE
    A real examination. The correct answer, the shortcut, the tips and
    the solution are all hidden until you submit. You get a timer,
    previous/next, a question-number grid showing what is answered and
    what is marked for review, a submit confirmation, and automatic
    submission when the timer reaches zero.

LEARNING MODE
    You answer one question at a time and everything is revealed
    immediately, always in this order:
        1. Correct / Incorrect - your answer and the correct answer
        2. Exam Shortcut       - the fastest way to get there
        3. Tips & Tricks       - traps, distractor patterns, memory aids
        4. Step-by-Step Solution
    Plus the topic's examiner pattern and must-know formulas.


THE MOCKS
---------
FULL YEAR MOCKS       Any one of the nine papers, complete, in the printed
                      order. Question order and option order are never
                      shuffled for a full paper.

SECTIONAL MOCKS       By unit. Pick one year (e.g. 2023 Probability), or
                      leave the year boxes empty to draw the unit from all
                      nine papers at once.

TOPIC MOCKS           Unit then topic - 45 syllabus topics.

SUBTOPIC MOCKS        Down to a single syllabus concept - 95 of them, for
                      example Bayes' Theorem, Runge-Kutta Method,
                      Simpson's One-Third Rule, Number Systems.

CUSTOM MOCK           Choose years, units, topics, subtopics, how many
                      questions, exam or learning mode, timer length, and
                      whether to shuffle the questions and the options.

PRACTICE MY MISTAKES  Every question you have answered wrongly, collected
                      automatically. A question leaves the bank once you
                      get it right. Questions you skipped are tracked
                      separately, so "Retry Unanswered" works too.


AFTER YOU SUBMIT
----------------
You get score, percentage, accuracy, attempted, correct, incorrect,
unanswered, total time and average time per attempted question, plus a
breakdown by unit, by topic and by question type, and a colour-coded map
of all the questions.

Then Review walks you through every question - your answer, the correct
answer, and the same four-part explanation - and you can filter the review
to just the ones you got wrong, or the ones you marked.


TRACKING YOUR PROGRESS
----------------------
ANALYTICS       Accuracy by year, unit, topic, subtopic and question type,
                a trend across your recent attempts, and a weak-area engine
                that names your weakest topic and offers a "Practice My
                Weak Areas" mock built from it.

ATTEMPT HISTORY Every mock you have submitted, and your progress across
                repeat attempts of the same mock.

BOOKMARKS       Flag any question with the star button and find it again
                under My Bookmarks.

SEARCH          Search all 1,470 questions - the 720 PYQs and the 750
                forecast problems - by text, year, unit, topic, subtopic or
                question id ("Bayes", "2023", "Regression", "Runge-Kutta",
                "2026-Q12", "FP-041"). Forecast hits carry the FORECAST
                badge so the two are never confused.


SETTINGS AND YOUR DATA
----------------------
Everything you do is stored only in your own browser, on your own device.
Nothing is uploaded anywhere, because the app never makes a network
request at all.

MARKING SCHEME  The source papers supplied for this build did not state a
                marking scheme or a paper duration, so the app does not
                pretend to know them. The defaults are +1 for a correct
                answer, 0 for a wrong answer, 0 for unanswered, and 1.5
                minutes per question (120 minutes for a full paper). Change
                any of them, including switching negative marking on, under
                Settings & Data.

EXPORT          Settings & Data > "Download progress JSON" saves a file
                with your history, bookmarks, mistakes and settings. If
                your browser blocks downloads from a local file, use
                "Show JSON to copy" instead and copy the text.

IMPORT          On the other device, open Settings & Data and either pick
                the file with "Import from file" or paste the copied text
                into the box and press "Import pasted JSON". This replaces
                the data already on that device.

RESET           Settings & Data has separate buttons to reset the attempt
                history, the mistake bank, the bookmarks, or everything.

IF SOMETHING LOOKS WRONG
------------------------
"Question database not loaded"
    questions.js is not next to index.html. Put all four files in the
    same folder.

Nothing happens when you double-click index.html
    Right-click it and choose "Open with" and then your browser.

Your history disappears after closing the browser
    You are probably in a private/incognito window, or your browser is set
    to block site data for local files. The app tells you at the top of
    the Home screen when this happens. Everything still works, but nothing
    is remembered. Use a normal window, or export your progress before
    closing.

The app looks cramped on a phone
    Turn the device sideways, or use a tablet. The layout works down to a
    360px-wide screen, but the exam screen has more room in landscape.


MATHEMATICS
-----------
Every formula in this app - in the authentic questions, in the forecast
questions, in the options, and in all the shortcuts, tips and solutions - is
written in LaTeX and typeset properly, the way a textbook sets it: real
fractions with rules, real integral and summation signs, proper subscripts and
superscripts, stretchy brackets, Greek letters and the correct spacing around
operators. The same is true of the six PDF volumes.

The typesetting engine is KaTeX. It is not downloaded when you open the app -
the engine, its stylesheet and all twenty of its mathematical fonts are
embedded inside index.html itself. That is why index.html is around 730 KB and
why the mathematics looks identical whether you are online, offline, or in
aeroplane mode.


A NOTE ON PRIVACY AND OFFLINE OPERATION
---------------------------------------
This application makes no network request of any kind. There is no CDN, no
API, no analytics, no externally loaded font, no external icon set, no
externally loaded JavaScript library, no server and no database. The
mathematics is typeset by an engine embedded inside index.html, not by an
online formula service. You can put the device in aeroplane mode and
everything, including the entire question bank, still works.
